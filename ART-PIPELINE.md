# ART-PIPELINE.md — how the cast gets drawn and built

The working plan for the art, so a new session can pick it up cold. Read
CLAUDE.md first (the rules and the state); this is the HOW and the QUEUE.

## The division of work (the owner's)

- **The owner draws** in ChatGPT: six images per character, from prompts
  Claude writes. Claude never waits on art it can prepare for.
- **Claude** writes the prompts (shown IN FULL in chat, in code blocks, ready
  to paste: the owner needs to copy them, not open files), saves the sheets
  the owner sends into the kit, builds the puppet, stages it, keeps the docs
  current, commits and pushes every step to the session's branch.
- **Order of work:** "write the next prompts before you build, so the images
  come while you work". When sheets arrive: save them, commit, hand over the
  NEXT character's prompts, then build.
- **Scope (the owner):** characters and rooms only. Game rules and features
  (the hidden JFK and Phoenix events, online play) are handled in other
  threads; do not change game code for them here.
- **Style lock:** every prompt is generated by `art-tools/prompts/poses.py`
  with `"style_lock": true`: attach Lancelot's character sheet (layout) and
  Washington's pose sheet A (style) to the first prompt; from the second on,
  the character's own sheet, their pose sheet A, and Washington's sheet A.
  The owner rejected drift toward detailed illustration (Davy Jones, Merlin).
  **An animal** takes Cerberus's pose sheet A as the style image instead
  (`style_ref` / `style_sheet` in the spec; the Cheshire Cat, after his first
  sheet came out as furry illustration against Washington's).
- **The gothic style (the owner, at the Headless Horseman: the flat cartoon
  "looks childish ... since Cheshire"; "split the difference" with the dark
  table mock-ups).** A spec's `style` / `style_short` replace the style words:
  bold outlines and readable shapes kept, adult proportions, deep 3-4 tone
  shading, candlelit rim light, rich muted colour. The first prompt takes its
  MOOD from `art-tools/style/gothic_mood.png` (the owner's mock-up; the prompt
  forbids its Universal monster, its pumpkin and its interface). The Horseman
  is the test: his sheet is APPROVED (the owner's tweaks: plate armour, skull
  bosses, a jagged-toothed carving) and is the style image from here on.
  **Settled (the owner: "Gothic Style yes"): from Transylvania on** (its cast
  and the Station's). Van Helsing, the Wolf Man and the Cheshire Cat are to be
  redrawn in it later (FIX-LATER.md); the hidden guests keep the presidents'
  cartoon (JFK sits at the White House; Loki is met mostly at early tables).
- **Make the next characters as they are needed, then go back and fix**
  (the owner). Fixes owed are kept in `FIX-LATER.md`, not in anyone's memory.
- **Lean into stop motion** (the owner): more drawn whole-figure poses rather
  than more bones. New kits put the big win and the crushing loss inside their
  24 poses (JFK and Loki: B3 and D4) and draw empty hands (`"empty_hands":
  true` in the spec: the game's flights move the cards and chips).

## One character, start to finish

The six images: character sheet, pose sheets A-D (24 poses, 3x2 each), face
overlay sheet. Kit folder: `art-tools/<id>_parts/`. A seventh, the reactions
sheet E (rest, the big win with hands raised, the crushing loss; prompt at the
end of the White House kits' POSES.md), gives the big win and loss; until it
is drawn the table uses the small win and the old beats. After any anim.json
change, re-run `python3 art-tools/build/win_small.py`,
`fallback_clips.py`, `tell_hold.py` and `life.py` (all in art-tools/build/),
then **`python3 art-tools/build/puppet_audit.py`**: every puppet held to one
list (life, the clips the table asks for, win/lose, a cue for every tell and
idle line, holds, seated frames, half-size images); it must print 0.
A whole-figure frame's body must reach the table line, or the room shows
under it and the figure floats (Lincoln's did: `lincoln_parts/frame_seat.py`).

1. **Prompts.** Write `<id>_parts/poses_spec.json` (copy a recent one:
   `scipio_parts`, `pope_parts`; a non-human body uses `replace`, see
   `cerberus_parts`, `phoenix_parts`), then
   `python3 art-tools/prompts/poses.py art-tools/<id>_parts/poses_spec.json art-tools/<id>_parts/POSES.md`.
   Tells and idles come from `data/characters/<id>.json`: each tell drawn
   beside the idle (decoy) it hides among. Paste every prompt into chat.
2. **Save** the sheets as `sources/<id>_sheet.png`, `poses_a.png` ...
   `poses_d.png`, `face_overlays.png`. Identify each sheet by its poses (the
   owner sends them in any order). Commit.
3. **Cut the face sheet:** `<id>_parts/cut_face.json` =
   `{"sheet": "sources/face_overlays.png", "stem": "<id>_face", "split": []}`,
   `python3 art-tools/cut_sheet.py art-tools/<id>_parts/cut_face.json`, then
   read `<id>_face/_contact_sheet.png` to number the pieces.
4. **Find the poses:** `python3 art-tools/build/pose_boxes.py art-tools/<id>_parts`
   then `python3 art-tools/build/corner_regs.py art-tools/<id>_parts`
   (boxes and regs into `poses_found.json`).
5. **Write `pose_rig.json`** from a similar kit (Arthur: a man with a prop at
   his side; Green Knight: a giant, `scale` 1.25 `origin_x` 225; Cerberus: a
   non-human). Every pose but A1 is a whole-figure frame, `"head": "own"`;
   `props` polygons round chips; arms (forearm + hand from the rest pose)
   polygons drawn on a gridded crop of A1. The master head is face piece 01
   placed with `place` (find it with `art-tools/build/fit_master.py`), or the
   rest pose's own head (Cerberus). `face.json` maps face pieces to layers;
   fits are cached in `<id>_rig/face/fits.json` (fix bad fits by hand: one
   scale for a whole set, as Spartacus's mouths).
   **The newer sheets draw a gold-orange rail**, the colour of skin and gold
   cloth. Start from Spartacus (bare skin), Scipio (gold bracers crossing
   the rail) or the Pope (gold embroidery, red robes): `rail_orange`
   `{"red": 120, "blue": 24}` (and `"green"`, default 70: Holmes in the cap), `rail_blue` 40, and as needed `rail_green`,
   `rail_long`, `no_wood`, a narrow `rail_band`. Their READMEs say why.
6. **Build:** `python3 art-tools/pose_rig.py art-tools/<id>_parts/pose_rig.json`
   (validate line: under ~1% of pixels differ), then
   `python3 art-tools/build/frames_sheet.py <id>` and LOOK at every frame:
   leftover drawn rail/felt, cut-off props, placement against the rest outline.
7. **Clips:** copy a kit's `clips.py` (e.g. `arthur_parts/clips.py`) to
   `<id>_parts/clips.py`, set the 24 clip names, notes, `cues` (each tell and
   idle TEXT from the character file -> its clip) and `fidgets`; run it.
8. **Publish:** `python3 art-tools/web_half.py <id>`; add the puppet to
   `PUPPETS` in `web/puppet/cast.ts`; `npm run check:data` (holds cues to the
   data); `node web/build.mjs`; check the bench at `#/puppet/<id>` with
   `node art-tools/build/bench_shots.cjs <id> /tmp/x bet all_in ...`.
9. **Docs:** a README in the kit (build commands, the kit's quirks, clip
   list), CLAUDE.md's CURRENT STATE paragraph for the table. Commit, push.

**A table goes live on the real screen only when its room and every one of
its puppets exist** (`ROOMS` and `PUPPETS` in `web/puppet/cast.ts`).
Preview a room with any built cast: serve `web/` (`PORT=5181 node web/serve.mjs`)
and `node art-tools/build/room_shot.cjs <room> out.png`. Once a table's cast is
built, `node art-tools/build/table_shot.cjs <table> out.png` shows it as the
game stages it (with a save that has cleared the tables before it).

## Rooms

Prompts: `art-tools/prompts/rooms.mjs` -> ROOM-ART-PROMPTS.md. Two images: a
21:9 backdrop (attach the White House backdrop for its camera) and a pieces
sheet (flames, story pieces). Give EXACT positions for anything that moves:
seats sit at 19, 34, 50, 66, 80% across, the gaps at 27, 42, 58, 73%
(Rome's first plate hid both braziers behind seats 1 and 4). Build with
`art-tools/rooms/<room>/pieces.json` + `python3 art-tools/room_web.py <room>`,
add it to `ROOMS` in `web/puppet/cast.ts`.
