# The Green Knight (after his return): every pose, and the prompts

The Green Knight after his return (see `art-tools/green_knight_parts/POSES.md`): the same armour and props, but beaten, and meaner for it (the owner: 'more menacing, it should be obvious that he was defeated, without being graphic'). His helm is gone; his face is an ordinary weathered human one, pale, dark-haired and dark-bearded (no green skin: the owner ruled it out as too outlandish); a clean bandage at his neck where the blow landed; dented, scraped plate, a cracked shoulder plate, a torn mantle. He no longer grins: his warmth is gone, his laugh is low. The game swaps to this puppet when he comes back. He returns once, so C6 is his entrance and D6 his last exit.

Tags (MOTION-SPEC.md): as the helmed set, played colder: the holly branch rustling (strong) beside moss creeping along his sleeve; a low laugh, head back (bluffing) beside a thin, cold smile; running a thumb along the head of his axe (weak) beside measuring the table with a flat hand, as if for a blow.

## Prompts

One fresh ChatGPT chat for all six. The first prompt (the return sheet) attaches TWO images: the Green Knight's helmed character sheet (`art-tools/green_knight_parts/sources/green_knight_sheet.png`) for his armour and layout, and Washington's pose sheet A for the style. From the second prompt on, attach the return sheet, this set's pose sheet A once it exists, AND Washington's pose sheet A.

### Character sheet

```
Character sheet for 2D animation rigging: the Green Knight after he has lost and come back, from the 14th-century poem Sir Gawain and the Green Knight. Copy the LAYOUT and the ARMOUR of the FIRST attached sheet (the same knight, the same green plate edged with gold, the same holly branch and axe). Take the STYLE from the SECOND attached image.

What has changed: he was beaten, and it shows, without anything graphic. His helm is GONE. His bare head: an ordinary, weathered human face, pale skin (NOT green), dark wild hair to his jaw, a dark short beard, heavy brows, hard eyes, a grim mouth; menacing, cold, no smile. A clean white linen bandage wound round his neck. His armour is battered: a few big, simple dents in the plates, one shoulder plate cracked, his green mantle torn at the edge (the damage drawn as a few bold shapes in the same flat style, not as fine scratches or grime). The holly branch a little bare, a few leaves gone. He is still enormous, a head taller than any other man at the table. No blood, no open wounds, no gore. Never holding a severed head. Nothing from the film The Green Knight (2021).

Pure white background (#FFFFFF). Every piece separate, with a closed dark outline and a wide gap all round it; nothing touches or overlaps. No text, no labels, no frames, no cast shadows.

The angle: a gentle three-quarter view, turned about 25 degrees from front-on, facing the viewer's right. Both eyes, the whole nose, the mouth and both shoulders clearly visible. NOT profile.

Draw, all facing the viewer's right, at the same size:
- His bare head four times, each with the bandaged neck and the top of the armour: neutral, grim; talking, mouth open mid-word; a low laugh, head back; a thin, cold smile
- The battered torso: no head, cut off straight below the waist, the upper arms down to the elbows; NO forearms, NO hands
- Two forearms in battered green plate with the gauntlets, lying on a table, pointing toward its centre, open at the elbow end
- Four seated reference poses, waist up behind the curved edge of a round green poker table, forearms resting on its edge, gauntlets apart, the holly branch tucked behind his right shoulder (the left of the picture), the head of the axe resting on the table's edge by his right hand: neutral, grim; the holly rustling; a low laugh; running his thumb along the axe head

STYLE, the most important rule: match the SECOND attached image (Washington, from the first table) exactly: simple, chunky cartoon shapes; bold, even dark outlines; smooth flat colour with ONE soft shadow tone; bright, clean, saturated colour; a face with only a few lines (no fine wrinkles, no pores, no cross-hatching); hair and beard drawn as a few big smooth locks, never strand by strand; cloth and metal with a few big folds or highlights, no texture, no stitching, no grime, no scratches. Readable at thumbnail size. NOT a detailed illustration, NOT painterly, NOT gritty, NOT realistic.
```
