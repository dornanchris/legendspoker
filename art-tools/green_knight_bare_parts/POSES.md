## Prompts

One fresh ChatGPT chat for all five (there is no new character sheet). The first prompt (pose sheet A) attaches THREE images: the Green Knight's helmed character sheet, the bare-head sheet (`art-tools/green_knight_parts/sources/bare_head_ref.png`, the owner's first try: its heads only), and Washington's pose sheet A for the style. From the second prompt on, attach the helmed character sheet, the bare-head sheet, this set's pose sheet A once it exists, AND Washington's pose sheet A.

### Pose sheet A

```
Character pose sheet for 2D animation reference: the Green Knight, exactly as on the attached character sheet (the same enormous knight in bright green plate armour edged with gold, but WITHOUT his helm: his bare head is the green-skinned, green-bearded, grinning one on the attached head sheet, with a healed red scar across the side of his neck; the holly branch tucked behind his right shoulder, the head of his great axe resting on the table's edge by his right hand, colours, line weight, flat cartoon style).

Draw 6 poses in a grid, 3 across and 2 down, in this order. Every pose: waist up, seated behind the curved edge of a round green poker table, three-quarter view facing the viewer's right, the same size, in the same place in its cell, with the table edge at the same height in every cell. Leave a little white between him and the cell's edges, the top of the holly branch included: nothing of him is cut off. Nothing below the table edge. No chair: nothing at all behind him, just white.

IMPORTANT for every pose: the arm that the pose does not mention stays EXACTLY as in pose 1, same position, same hand. No helm in any pose: his bare green head, as on the attached head sheet. The holly branch stays tucked behind his right shoulder (the left of the picture), standing up above his head, and the axe head stays on the table's edge by his right hand, its haft going down out of sight, unless the pose says otherwise. Keep the simple, chunky, flat cartoon style of the attached sheets and of Washington's sheet: few lines, big smooth shapes, flat colour, no fine detail or texture.

1. At rest: both forearms lying along the table's edge, his gauntlets resting on the felt in front of him, a hand's width apart, relaxed and NOT touching, NOT laced, NOT overlapping; the holly branch behind his right shoulder; the axe head on the table's edge by his right hand; bareheaded, a big friendly grin, mouth closed
2. Peeking at his cards: his right hand cupped over two cards on the felt, thumb lifting the near corner, eyes down
3. Checking: one big finger of his right hand tapping the felt
4. Calling: his right hand sliding a short stack of chips forward
5. Betting: his right hand shoving a stack of chips well forward
6. Folding: his right hand tossing two cards away face down, with a shrug

Cards have plain dark backs. Pure white background around each pose; poses do not touch. No text, no numbers, no labels. No helm, no horse, no severed head, no gore, no bark or tree skin, no chair.
```
